1. The East region generated the highest revenue share in Q3 (approx. 32%), making it our strongest regional market, whereas the South region lagged behind and requires targeted promotional support.
2. Online sales showed steady month-over-month growth, outperforming offline channels in August and September, indicating a consumer shift toward digital retail platforms.
3. The top 5 products (led by consumer electronics and home items) account for nearly 40% of total revenue; inventory stock for these specific items must be prioritized for next quarter.
4. High-value repeat customers (those purchasing more than 10 times) drive a significant portion of cash flow, suggesting we should introduce a structured VIP loyalty program.
5. Category-wise trend analysis indicates that the 'Electronics' category is rising rapidly, while 'Clothing' experiences fluctuating sales with a slight dip toward the end of the quarter.
6. Average Order Value (AOV) remains stable across offline channels, but online orders feature slightly higher basket sizes due to bundled product offers.
7. Strategic Recommendation: Reallocate a portion of the marketing budget toward the South region while continuing to expand digital ad placement to capitalize on online sales momentum.
